# KamLife Marketing Doctrine (locked 2026-07-07)

## Identity, not software
Clients rename themselves into the brand ("Goddess_Zee Kamlife", "Beauty
KamLife"). KamLife is a belonging system. Market membership in something, not
features of an app.

## Coach K is the product; Kam is the moat
- NEVER market "AI" — category race to zero. Coach K is a coach who happens to
  be always available; the AI is plumbing.
- The cloned-voice Sunday recap is the uncopyable weapon: competitors can add a
  chatbot; they cannot add Kam's voice and credibility. Founder-as-moat:
  voice everywhere, face on the landing page, build-in-public story
  ("a coach who got tired of watching clients fail built one that never sleeps").

## The ICP is Natasha (real client, 2026-07-06)
29, 90kg, fat loss, treadmill at home, no gym, WFH sedentary, beginner,
insecurities front-loaded in one paste. She will never download MyFitnessPal or
enter a gym. The competitor is shame + doing nothing, not apps.

## The two lines — everything is a variation of these
- Mechanic: **"You don't track. It remembers."**
- Soul: **"The coach that never makes you feel like a failure."**
  (Structurally true — anti-shame is coded into the prompt and enforced by
  guardrails. The diet industry runs on shame; we are its opposite.)

## Distribution: WhatsApp Status is Times Square
Unit of virality = one screenshot travelling one social graph. Every artifact
must be status-shaped (vertical, bold, brand footer — the Week Card is the
template). Later: stokvel mechanics — pods of 5 friends, group accountability.
SA fitness spreads through church/work/family WhatsApp groups, not app stores.

## Price against trainers, never against free apps
R199/month = R6.60/day, less than a taxi, ~5% of a personal trainer.
"A real coach used to be for rich people. Not anymore."
NEVER discount (cheap coach = worthless coaching). Referrals pay in product:
a free month, not rands.

## Next 14 days (parallel to trust-engine drilling)
1. Two lines locked; zero feature talk anywhere.
2. Meta/WhatsApp own number finished (HUMAN_TASKS #4-8) — distribution prereq.
3. Artifact folder: every clean conversation, week card, transformation. Launch inventory.
4. Next 10 users from status, white-gloved (bulk-intake build supports this).
5. Harvest 3 tension-arc stories over 6-8 weeks (problem → coach catches it →
   outcome). Stories spread; demos die by the third post.
6. R0 on ads until a referred user refers someone.

## The gate
None of this fires until drill sessions come back clean. A shame-free coach
that misremembers dinner is a broken promise in a screenshot — and screenshots
are the whole newspaper.
