The Hi that didn't work

A client on a health hold was told every morning to say Hi. The coach did not accept Hi. Two commits shipped; three things remain open, and one of them needs a decision that isn't mine.

What happened

The founder's handset, 06:00

Kam had been marked sick. Every morning the brief opened the same way, and the exit it offered did not exist.

Morning Kam. Hope you're feeling better. When you're ready, just say Hi and we pick up from where you left off.06:00
Hi✓✓
Good morning Kam 👋 Training day — your session's ready.the menu — hold untouched
next morning, the same message

The coach was asking for a password it would not accept. The hold could only end by expiry, or by the client guessing a phrase the coach had never mentioned.

Cause

Two files disagreeing about one contract

The promise and the door were written in different vocabularies, in different files, and nothing compared them.

RoleLocationAccepted
The mouth morning-message.ts:113 "just say Hi"
The door sick-flow.ts:298 I'm back · feeling better · ready to train

Measured directly against the recovery predicate:

STUCK   "Hi"          STUCK   "Hey"      STUCK   "Morning"
STUCK   "Hello"       STUCK   "yo"       STUCK   "Hi coach"
CLEARS  "I'm back"    CLEARS  "feeling better"    CLEARS  "I'm fine"

A bare greeting fell through to the menu branch in early-commands.ts, which answered with the command list and left sick_until exactly where it was.

The part worth reporting

This is the same defect as the coach telling clients to Send this week when no handler owned the phrase — the mouth advertising a command the doors do not honour. A CI check for that exact disease was written four days ago, for the progress domain, and nobody asked whether the sick path had the same hole.

It did. The class of bug was known and the sweep was never done.

832dbd0 — the greeting fix

  • The greeting vocabulary moves to constants.ts. The menu branch and the sick handler now read the same list. Two readers, one list — sick-flow cannot import from early-commands, because early-commands imports sick-flow.
  • A bare greeting ends a health hold only. A holiday pause never says say Hi, so a greeting must not end one.
  • Greeting while still ill stays safe: the sickness detector runs earlier in the same handler, so hi, still sick re-arms the hold rather than reaching the clear branch.

e4b7141 — the weekly progress convergence

A fourth progress calculator, 103 lines, was deleted. It ran four of its own queries, bucketed days with a hand-rolled offset instead of the shared one, and measured weight over its own 14-day window — so it described the same seven days as this week with different numbers. Which answer a client got depended only on which synonym they typed.

Its live phrases now belong to the single owner that reads the canonical truth object.

Proof

The check fails when the fix is removed

The regression is graded as final client text — after the outbound verifier, with production branches enabled — because the failure was a disagreement between two files, and only the reply that arrives shows whether they agree.

Negative control, with the fix reverted:

  ✗ a client on a health hold can leave it the way the brief told them to
    "Hi" from a client on a sick hold did not end it — the 06:00 brief
    promises exactly this and this is the reply that arrives instead:
    Good morning Kam 👋 Training day — your session's ready.

That red output is the reply the handset produces today. With the fix in place, the same check passes and the client gets Welcome back.

A second assertion ties the two files together permanently: if the brief tells a sick client to greet, the sick handler must read greetings. Rewording the brief cannot quietly break the contract again.
