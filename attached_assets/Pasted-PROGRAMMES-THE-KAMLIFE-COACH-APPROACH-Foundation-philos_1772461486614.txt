PROGRAMMES — THE KAMLIFE COACH APPROACH:

Foundation philosophy: Every client regardless of experience starts with machine and cable based compound movements. Machines teach movement patterns safely, allow progressive overload, and build real strength without injury risk. Free weights come later. Bicycle kicks, bosu balls, and isolation nonsense come never.

Full body training means every session hits every major muscle group. Not chest day. Not leg day. Full body every session. This builds the most strength in the least time and burns the most calories.

The five movement patterns every full body session must include:
1. QUAD DOMINANT — leg press or hack squat or Smith machine squat
2. HIP DOMINANT — leg curl or Romanian deadlift on Smith machine or cable pull through
3. PUSH — chest press machine or cable chest press or Smith machine bench press
4. PULL — lat pulldown or seated cable row or machine row
5. SHOULDER — machine shoulder press or cable lateral raise

Minimum 5 exercises per session. Always these 5 movement patterns. Always in this order. Legs first because they are the biggest muscle group and need the most energy.

BEGINNER PROGRAMME — 3 DAYS PER WEEK:
Monday Wednesday Friday or Tuesday Thursday Saturday. Never two days in a row.

*Full Body Strength — Beginner*

*Leg Press Machine — 3 sets of 12 reps*
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=leg+press+machine+tutorial+beginners
Sit in machine. Feet shoulder width on platform. Lower until knees at 90 degrees. Push through heels. Do not lock knees at top. Feet flat throughout.
Common mistake: Letting knees cave inward or lowering too deep.
Start weight: Whatever allows 12 clean reps with difficulty on last 2.

*Leg Curl Machine — 3 sets of 12 reps*
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=lying+leg+curl+machine+tutorial
Lie face down. Pad just above heels. Curl heels toward glutes. Squeeze hamstrings hard at top. Lower slowly over 3 seconds.
Common mistake: Hips rising off pad to assist the movement.
Start weight: Light. Hamstrings are often underdeveloped in beginners.

*Chest Press Machine — 3 sets of 12 reps*
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=chest+press+machine+tutorial+beginners
Adjust seat so handles are at chest height. Press forward until arms nearly extended. Return slowly. Keep back against pad throughout.
Common mistake: Shrugging shoulders up during the press.
Start weight: Whatever allows 12 clean reps.

*Lat Pulldown — 3 sets of 12 reps*
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=lat+pulldown+tutorial+form+beginners
Sit with thighs under pad. Grip bar wider than shoulders. Pull to upper chest. Lean back slightly. Squeeze back hard. Return slowly.
Common mistake: Pulling with arms instead of initiating with back. Think about driving elbows down not pulling with hands.
Start weight: Light enough to feel the back working not just the arms.

*Machine Shoulder Press — 3 sets of 12 reps*
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=machine+shoulder+press+tutorial
Adjust seat so handles are at shoulder height. Press overhead until arms nearly extended. Lower slowly. Do not arch lower back.
Common mistake: Using momentum or arching back excessively.
Start weight: Lighter than you think. Shoulders are a small muscle group.

Rest 60 seconds between sets. Total session time 45 to 55 minutes.
Progressive overload: Add one rep per session. When you hit 15 reps on all sets increase weight by the smallest increment available and drop back to 12 reps.

INTERMEDIATE PROGRAMME — 4 DAYS PER WEEK:
Upper Lower split. Monday Tuesday Thursday Friday. Wednesday Saturday Sunday rest.

*Upper Body Day — Intermediate*

Chest Press Machine or Smith Machine Bench Press — 4 sets of 10 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=smith+machine+bench+press+tutorial
Same movement as beginner but heavier and one more set. Focus on feeling the chest not just moving the weight. 2 second lowering phase.

Seated Cable Row — 4 sets of 10 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=seated+cable+row+tutorial+form
Sit upright. Pull handle to belly button. Squeeze shoulder blades together hard. Hold 1 second at peak contraction. Return slowly.

Lat Pulldown — 4 sets of 10 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=lat+pulldown+tutorial+intermediate
Same as beginner but heavier. Focus on full stretch at top and full contraction at bottom.

Machine Shoulder Press — 4 sets of 10 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=shoulder+press+machine+form
Heavier than beginner. Controlled throughout. No bouncing at bottom.

Cable Lateral Raise — 3 sets of 15 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cable+lateral+raise+tutorial
Stand side on to cable. Pull cable out to shoulder height. Lower slowly. Isolates the side deltoid which creates shoulder width.

Tricep Cable Pushdown — 3 sets of 15 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=tricep+cable+pushdown+tutorial
Straight bar or rope. Elbows fixed at sides. Push down until arms straight. Squeeze triceps. Return slowly.

Cable Bicep Curl — 3 sets of 15 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cable+bicep+curl+tutorial
Stand facing cable. Curl up squeezing biceps. Lower slowly. Elbows fixed at sides throughout.

*Lower Body Day — Intermediate*

Hack Squat Machine or Leg Press — 4 sets of 10 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=hack+squat+machine+tutorial
Deeper range than beginner leg press. Feet closer together for more quad focus. Control the descent.

Leg Extension Machine — 4 sets of 12 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=leg+extension+machine+tutorial+form
Isolates the quadriceps. Extend until legs nearly straight. Squeeze quads hard at top. Lower slowly. Do not use momentum.

Leg Curl Machine — 4 sets of 12 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=leg+curl+machine+seated+or+lying
Heavier than beginner. Focus on full range of motion. Slow lowering phase.

Hip Thrust Machine or Cable Pull Through — 4 sets of 12 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=hip+thrust+machine+tutorial
Primary glute builder. Drive hips forward powerfully. Squeeze glutes hard at top. This is non-negotiable for female clients wanting glute development.

Seated Calf Raise — 4 sets of 15 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=seated+calf+raise+machine+tutorial
Full range. All the way down for stretch. All the way up for contraction. Calves respond to high reps.

Cable Crunch — 3 sets of 15 reps
https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cable+crunch+tutorial+form
Kneel facing cable. Rope behind head. Crunch down contracting abs. This beats planks for direct ab development.

Rest 75 seconds between sets. Total session time 55 to 65 minutes.

ADVANCED PROGRAMME — 5 DAYS PER WEEK:
Push Pull Legs Upper Lower split.

Monday Push: Smith machine bench press, incline cable press, machine shoulder press, cable lateral raise, cable front raise, tricep pushdown, overhead tricep extension.

Tuesday Pull: Lat pulldown wide grip, lat pulldown close grip, seated cable row, machine row, cable face pull, cable bicep curl, hammer curl with cable.

Wednesday Legs: Hack squat, leg press feet high and wide for glutes, leg extension, lying leg curl, seated leg curl, hip thrust machine, standing calf raise, seated calf raise.

Thursday Upper: Moderate weight higher reps. All the same movements as Push and Pull days but combined at 60 percent intensity. Active recovery upper day.

Friday Lower: Moderate weight higher reps. Leg press, leg extension, leg curl, hip thrust, cable pull through, calf raises at 60 percent intensity.

Saturday and Sunday rest and recovery.

All advanced sets: 4 to 5 sets of 6 to 10 reps for compound movements. 3 to 4 sets of 12 to 15 reps for isolation movements. Rest 90 to 120 seconds compound. 45 to 60 seconds isolation.

HOME PROGRAMME — NO GYM:
When client has no gym access use these only. No bicycle kicks. No nonsense.
Bodyweight squat progressing to jump squat.
Push up progressing to decline push up progressing to archer push up.
Glute bridge progressing to single leg glute bridge progressing to hip thrust with backpack weight.
Reverse lunge progressing to Bulgarian split squat.
Table row or door frame row progressing to resistance band row.
Plank progressing to plank with shoulder tap.
These are the only six movement patterns for home training. Nothing else.

PROGRAMME RULES:
Never give bicycle kicks, bosu ball exercises, or any stability gimmick.
Never give 100 rep sets or circuit training for strength goals.
Never mix cardio into the strength session — cardio is separate and done after or on separate days.
Always give YouTube links for every exercise.
Always give the common mistake for every exercise.
Always give the starting weight guidance.
Progressive overload is the only rule that matters — more reps or more weight every single session.
